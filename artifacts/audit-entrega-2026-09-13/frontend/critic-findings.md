## Findings

1. **P1: correction from patient A remains under patient B's clinical timeline.** `apps/web/src/features/clinical/Clinical.tsx:95–109,260`. `setAddenda` is shared across encounters, is not cleared before switching to another signed document, and no request-generation guard rejects late responses. Actual synthetic browser: open A, obtain its correction, open B while B addenda response is pending; heading is `LINHA DO TEMPO · Patient B` while A's correction is still visible. This is clinical-context misinformation, not proven cross-ACL exfiltration. PRD UC02 lines90/98 requires patient history with references/context. Closure: key addenda by document/encounter, clear pending view, ignore stale responses, explicit per-resource errors; reproduce rapid A/B switches with reversed responses and prove zero wrong-patient text.

2. **P1: older signed-document corrections disappear from the chart.** `Clinical.tsx:104–109,230,260`. Initial load selects only newest signed document and fetches only its addenda; adding to an older document temporarily replaces the shared list, but revisit/reload reverts to newest. Actual synthetic browser with a-old and a-new signed docs requested only `/clinical/documents/a-new/addenda`. PRD FR05 line201 and UC02 line98 require adenda and timeline. Closure: complete document-associated addenda retrieval/navigation, show document/author/time, test two signed documents with corrections and full reload. Failure branch at97 also silently reports no addenda on API errors; distinguish unavailable from empty.

3. **P1: pharmacy/estoque cannot reach authorized medication dispensing journey.** `Internacao.tsx:72,106–114,299`; `Clinical.tsx:82–84`; domain `packages/domain/src/index.ts:1338,1517,1522`. Only medication-order dispensing button is nested in hospitalization, whose mandatory fetches forbid estoque. Clinical parent likewise fetches forbidden encounters/documents before the tab. Stock has movement listing but no medication-order dispensing request. Actual isolated in-memory domain probe: estoque allowed listStock and listMedicationOrders; FORBIDDEN listBeds, listHospitalEpisodes, listEncounters. Browser-level role flow not run by this critic. PRD persona line59 and UC05 dispensing require this user journey. Closure: authorized pharmacy entry/reads independent of broader clinical data; end-to-end estoque dispense against an active prescription with receipt, stock reduction, and scoped data denial intact.

4. **P2: planned hospitalization is an admitted dead end.** `Internacao.tsx:292,307–311`; domain `index.ts:1536,1591,1615`. Form offers no bed and produces PLANNED; row offers neither assign bed nor admit. Domain also has no bed assignment transition in inspected hospital contract; status refuses admission with no bed, discharge refuses PLANNED. Source inspection, high confidence; no browser transition mutation. Closure: implement/review a complete assign/admit or cancel path and prove same episode retains its identity and history after a bed becomes available. PRD UC04 lines120,128.

5. **P2: signed-content modal opens without moving focus inside.** `Clinical.tsx:33–36,280–281`. Initial selector chooses disabled title field; calling focus does nothing and there is no enabled-element fallback. Actual synthetic browser active element remains underlying `Ver conteúdo` button, dialogContains=false. Signed content itself is disabled textarea (not keyboard focusable). Closure: focus enabled close/control or focusable heading; present readable content with keyboard navigation/copy where allowed; verify focus entry, tab containment, Escape and restoration for every read-only dialog and supported browser/viewport. Frozen bar F22 requires real accessibility proof. This is a concrete focus failure, not a claimed full WCAG audit.
